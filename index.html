    How to Play

    Trace words. Drag (or use arrow keys + Enter) across a straight line of letters — horizontal, vertical, or diagonal — to spell a word from the list.
    Both directions. Words may read forwards or backwards along the line.
    Clean streaks. Invalid selections reset your clean bonus. Each word scores 20 points per letter, plus clean, speed, and daily-streak bonuses.
    Keyboard. ↑ ↓ ← → move the cursor, Enter/Space starts or confirms a line, Esc cancels or pauses, H gives a hint, C resets the camera.
